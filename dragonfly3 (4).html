Dragonfly 3 — flight study

New controller: acceleration, finite turns, hovering, optional glides and braking before landing. Pokes redirect moving insects without resetting velocity or accumulated wing phase. Flight paths and Motion inspector show recent positions and first-insect state; Study tempo slows the whole simulation.

Four shared photographic assets and eight species-inspired color presets are retained. Color presets are not species-specific anatomy or flight models. The two eyes remain fixed to the head; optional small head motion is retained. Bank, pitch and wing feathering are 2.5D image projections.

These are authored animation parameters, not universal biological constants: carrier about 28 Hz; hindwing-lead targets about 17 degrees during escape, 95 during transit and 155 during hover/braking, blended continuously. Wings do not generate calculated physical forces. Movement uses a planar steering controller, not CFD or six-degree-of-freedom dynamics.

Percher and Patrol are generic styles with future rest intervals of 7–20 and 2–7 simulation seconds. Flight seconds is a minimum before final approach; glides, hovering and approach can extend it. Size/count changes can settle and reposition insects. Personal-space yielding is not a collision solver. Browser display maps are not desktop overlay windows; native adapter protocol 2 remains available with added motion fields.

Research inspiration retained from the design work: Li et al. (2018), DOI 10.1038/s41598-018-19237-w; Noda et al. (2023), DOI 10.1017/jfm.2023.471; Wakeling and Ellington (1997), DOI 10.1242/jeb.200.3.543. Simulation settings are illustrative, not fitted to those experiments.

All assets are embedded. Open this single HTML file directly; no builder, file selection, server or other file is needed. Hidden tabs suspend motion and reduced-motion preferences start paused. Browser visuals, compositing, motion blur and device performance require browser testing; numerical/mock tests do not validate actual GLSL output.
